# Local Business Signals Criteria

These three criteria only apply to local businesses. For non-local
businesses, each automatically scores 5/10, since the signal doesn't
meaningfully apply. These are the criteria that push a local business's
maximum possible score from 150 to 180.

## Local Entity Signals (0-10)

Is the NAP (Name, Address, Phone) clearly stated? Are service areas,
city names, or geographic identifiers explicitly mentioned throughout?
Does the page reinforce a Google Business Profile entity?

- 0-3: No local entity signals. NAP absent or inconsistent.
- 4-6: Some local signals present but incomplete — missing address,
  phone, or service area details.
- 7-10: Complete NAP visible, clear service areas named, local entity
  signals consistent throughout.

## Hyper-Local Content (0-10)

Does the page reference specific local landmarks, neighborhoods,
streets, zip codes, nearby cities, local events, or community-specific
details that signal genuine local presence to AI engines? Generic
city-name mentions alone do not qualify.

- 0-3: Only generic city name present. No neighborhood, landmark, or
  community-specific references.
- 4-6: Some local references present but surface-level — city and
  state only, no deeper geographic specificity.
- 7-10: Multiple hyper-local references — specific neighborhoods,
  landmarks, zip codes, nearby areas, or community-specific context
  that signals deep local presence.

## Local Citation Signals (0-10)

Does the page reference or link to external local citation sources —
Google Business Profile, Yelp, Angi, HomeAdvisor, BBB, Nextdoor, local
directories, or review platforms?

- 0-3: No mention of reviews, ratings, or external citation platforms.
- 4-6: Some review references present but no direct links or specific
  platform mentions.
- 7-10: Clear references to GBP, review platforms, star ratings, or
  local directories with specific citation signals that AI engines can
  verify.

A common fix: embed a visible Google Reviews rating with star count and
review number (e.g., "Rated 4.9/5 based on 850 Google reviews") with a
direct link to the Google Business Profile, plus Google Maps embeds for
each service area served.
