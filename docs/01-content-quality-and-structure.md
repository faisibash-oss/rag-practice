# Content Quality & Structure Criteria

These seven criteria measure whether a page's content is written and
structured in a way AI engines can easily read, extract, and quote from.

## Direct Answer Presence (0-10)

Does the page answer a likely AI query directly within the first 100
words — without requiring the reader to scroll?

- 0-3: No direct answer. Content is vague, promotional, or requires
  scrolling to find meaning.
- 4-6: An answer exists but is incomplete, buried, or unclear.
- 7-10: A specific, complete answer appears within the first paragraph.

## Speakable Summary (0-10)

Does the page have a clear, concise, standalone summary in the first
paragraph that could be read aloud as a complete answer? AI engines and
voice assistants pull from the first paragraph first.

- 0-3: First paragraph is promotional, vague, or requires context to
  understand.
- 4-6: A partial answer exists in the opening but is not complete or
  standalone.
- 7-10: First paragraph contains a complete, concise, self-contained
  answer that works perfectly as a spoken response.

A common fix here is adding a 2-3 sentence standalone summary directly
under the H1, before any images — one that states who the business is,
what it does, and who it serves, in plain readable sentences.

## Content Formatting (0-10)

Is the content structured with clear hierarchy — H2/H3 headings, bullet
points, numbered lists, tables — that allows AI engines to parse and
extract specific sections easily?

- 0-3: Wall of text. No clear hierarchy. No bullets, tables, or
  structured sections.
- 4-6: Some formatting present but inconsistent or shallow.
- 7-10: Clear heading hierarchy, bullets/tables where appropriate,
  scannable and parseable structure throughout.

## Semantic Completeness (0-10)

Does the page comprehensively cover the topic — including related
questions, comparisons, sub-topics, and adjacent concerns?

- 0-3: Narrow, thin coverage of only the core topic.
- 4-6: Core topic covered but related questions and sub-topics are
  missing.
- 7-10: Comprehensive coverage including related entities, comparisons,
  and adjacent questions a user might ask.

## Natural Language Alignment (0-10)

Is content written conversationally — matching how people actually
phrase queries to AI engines?

- 0-3: Formal, keyword-stuffed, or unnatural language throughout.
- 4-6: Mixed — some natural, some unnatural language.
- 7-10: Consistently conversational throughout. Matches natural query
  phrasing.

## FAQ Structure (0-10)

Are there real questions written the way humans ask AI engines, with
specific direct answers?

- 0-3: No FAQ section present.
- 4-6: FAQ exists but questions are not conversational or answers are
  vague.
- 7-10: Multiple natural-language questions with complete, specific
  answers beneath each.

## PAA Alignment (0-10)

Does the content address secondary and related questions that appear in
Google's People Also Ask boxes for this topic? PAA questions are shorter
and more specific than FAQ — they target adjacent user intent around the
main topic.

- 0-3: No PAA-style secondary questions addressed anywhere on the page.
- 4-6: Some related questions covered but not in concise PAA-friendly
  format.
- 7-10: Multiple PAA-style questions with short, direct answers that
  match the format AI engines and Google PAA boxes prefer.

A typical fix: add 5 PAA-style questions to the FAQ section, each with a
short, direct, specific answer — not a long explanatory paragraph.
